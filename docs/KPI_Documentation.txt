# Supply Chain KPI Documentation

## 1. Average Supplier Lead Time

**SQL Formula:**
```sql
ROUND(AVG(lead_time_days)::numeric, 2)
```

**Result:** 7.9 days

**Supplier Summary:**
- Total suppliers: 10
- Average lead time: 7.9 days
- Fastest supplier: SUP007 — 4 days
- Slowest supplier: SUP006 — 12 days

**Supplier Performance:**

| Supplier | Lead Time |
|---|---:|
| SUP007 | 4 days |
| SUP001 | 5 days |
| SUP004 | 6 days |
| SUP010 | 7 days |
| SUP002 | 7 days |
| SUP005 | 8 days |
| SUP008 | 9 days |
| SUP003 | 10 days |
| SUP009 | 11 days |
| SUP006 | 12 days |

**Description:** Measures the average number of days suppliers take to provide products. A lower lead time generally indicates faster supplier performance.

## 2. Critical Inventory Percentage

**Definition:** Percentage of products whose current stock is at or below safety stock.

**SQL condition:**
```sql
current_stock <= safety_stock
```

**Results:**
- Critical products: 26
- Total products: 118
- Critical inventory percentage: 22.03%

**Business Impact:** Identifies products requiring urgent replenishment to reduce the risk of stockouts.

## 3. Low Stock Percentage

**Definition:** Percentage of products whose current stock is above safety stock but at or below reorder point.

**SQL condition:**
```sql
current_stock > safety_stock
AND current_stock <= reorder_point
```

**Results:**
- Low-stock products: 20
- Total products: 118
- Low-stock percentage: 16.95%

**Business Impact:** Helps identify products that need replenishment before they become critical.

## 4. Sufficient Stock Percentage

**Definition:** Percentage of products whose current stock is above the reorder point.

**SQL condition:**
```sql
current_stock > reorder_point
```

**Results:**
- Sufficient-stock products: 72
- Total products: 118
- Sufficient-stock percentage: 61.02%

**Business Impact:** Shows the proportion of products currently above their replenishment threshold.
